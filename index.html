Plex Playlists is now QuickPlaylist

Same app, new name, and its own web address.

Open QuickPlaylist

  1. Sign in with Plex again at the new address.
  2. Add it to your home screen again.
  3. Remove the old Playlists icon from your home screen.

The old sign-in also still exists at Plex. To end it, remove “Plex Playlists” under Authorized Devices in your Plex account.
